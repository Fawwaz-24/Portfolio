01 · About

Who I am

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate currently pursuing my M.Tech in Computational & Experimental Mechanics at IIT Madras, with a CGPA of 9.08.

My work spans nonlinear FEA, advanced structural simulation, and precision CAD design — from modelling large-deformation wing deflections using Discrete Differential Geometry to running explicit dynamic impact simulations with user-defined VUMAT subroutines in Abaqus.

Prior to IIT Madras, I gained hands-on industrial experience at Larsen & Toubro's Nuclear Business Unit at BARC Tarapur, managing fabrication of nuclear-grade components and supervising critical welding processes including SMAW and GTAW.
